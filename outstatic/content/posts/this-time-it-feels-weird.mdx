The world is falling apart… trust is no longer automatically assumed to be there. Yet people keep living their life pretending like nothing happened. Sometimes, the ruling class implemented new rules and laws in silence so people wouldn’t have time to react or object. Your life is being surveilled and yet almost no one seems to care. The controlling tentacles are so invisible people forget to ask what the real world is. People become indifferent, apathetic, numb, depressed, lost and insane. They stop caring about their neighbors, they stop calling their friends, they live but dead inside.

Like this whole world looks crazy. After some huge mistakes in trusting the wrong people, now I have to say I’m a little big paranoid and see evilness everywhere and be prepared just in case. I know this isnt sustainable but how else can I protect myself if the people who are supposed to protect me let me down?

Now I’m scared of everything and everyone. I’m scared of the racist teenagers that I met in a local supermarket, who threw boxes of condoms at me again and no staff took me seriously. I’m scared of the random strangers or waiters that just yelled at me for no reason because I don’t understand what they say. I’m scared I would get stuck here forever but I’m also scared to go back and be abused again by my family. I’m scared of being broke, which may push me to rely on predatory people that traumatized me. I’m scared of my landlord recently for no reason as well even tho he has been nice and good to me so far. People switch up so quickly like what David did that I would never ever felt in peace or safe around people, even the nice ones again, especially men like you literally do not know when and how they will choose to hurt you if they want to. It’s sad. It’s sad how one person, or maybe not just one, but so many people reveal themselves as nothing like the kind and friendly mask they show to the world. The ex-flatmate that emotionally bullied me and spread rumors about me when I stood up to her psychological abusive remarks? The jobs that exhausted me and disrespected my well being many years ago? Like how could I even let that happen? Yes because my parents are abusive physically and emotionally. They taught me that dysfunction and disrespect is normal. I grew up believing the whole world was against me because why didn’t anyone, like just 1 single adult protect me from their tyranny? Why? Why? Why?

Now I no longer believe the world is against me, it’s just evil people do evil things and the rest just stands there watching without doing anything to protect the victims. Their lives carry on while we, the victims, suffer. It’s unfair, isn’s it? Why do I have to be hurt again and again and yet I see no consequences to these perpetrators? Maybe they do face their karma but of course they are not going to tell me. It comes in circle. Anyway, even tho I’m more knowledgeable now and know better how to take care of myself, I’m still afraid there are many victims like the younger version of me out there - feeling lost and betrayed by the silence of the world. It’s ironic isnt it when David literally inspired and empowered me to be the strong person that I am today but he was also the one that abandoned me in the process of me getting over the mess of my past. Maybe he was never meant to be there for me in the long run. It was kinda sad but what can I do? 😞

I wish life could go back to the way it was when I was at Bard, when I was happy and worried-free, when I still believe in the goodness of people because of him and when I still believe the future is bright ahead. Since then, things just spiraled downwards terribly. Even tho I have managed to rebuild a strong foundation for myself again - new job, new identity, new priorities, more stability, more hobbies, a life more aligned with my purpose and principles - yet I still feel how fragile everything was or still is. I don’t know why, everything seems fine but if there is one thing I have recognized the most in my entire life, when everything seems fine is when the most hidden destruction can appear out of nowhere. I’m not sure what this feeling is, whether it’s just a trauma response from the chaos 2025 or it’s my intuition telling me something or it’s both.

I will start with some concrete evidence of where I feel like something is off. First is the economy. People pretend like everything is fine yet everything is waiting in line to be exploded all at once: geopolitical tensions can lead to wars in various continents, inflation, deflation, currency debasement, AI, asset bubbles, unemployment rate, inequality, social injustice, sexism, national debts, pension funds running out, etc. Like we all know these things are coming, they are just delayed and most finance and tech bros and politicians are just in denial. I think I’ll be fine if an economic collapse happens coz I live in Bulgaria, it’s not as chaotic as the US and also a lot of US jobs are outsourced here and there are only 2M people in this city where I live, most of them don’t even speak English so it’s obvious to say there is not much competition in the job market but last year it was still very brutal for me to find a job because I need a company to apply for a residency for me. It took me a painful 9-month period but here I am. I wouldn’t say having a full-time job makes me feel any more secure about my future, it just feels easier to pay all my bills now, I’m relaxing but not hopeful anymore. My rent is 50% my income coz my toxic ex-flatmate left and I have been scared to find someone new. I also don’t wanna move coz I’m even more terrified to get scammed again by real estate agents and non-English speaking landlords that are just an annoying greedy piss on the face. It’s just too much risk and if I move I have to pay those brokers 1 month rent + 1 month deposit to the landlord that is guaranteed they will not give back so yeah could easily wipe out a few k already. And it’s not like a 1 bedroom is any cheaper, people are so greedy these days, I check around and most modern 1-bedroom apartments are only a few hundred eur cheaper. It makes no sense to pay thousands to save a few hundred.

Now it’s about people. I feel like life is so much heavier than what it used to be that people don’t even know how to communicate or share their struggles anymore. Instead people feel ashamed to say they are struggling so they never say it, even to their friends or acquaintances and plus most people are so materialistic these days, they would just laugh at you instead of trying to help anyway. See, what a broken society we have evolved into - disconnected, disintegrated, traumatized, displaced, distrust, etc. I do not wish to going back to the naive version of myself. Growing up in an abusive family + oppressive government + being naive about others’ motives is the worst combination ever. I would rather be mean than being taken advantage of again. But then it would steal me of my joy. I don’t know what to do… this society isnt safe to me. Europe, Bulgaria, Vietnam… I don’t know where to go. Everyone and everywhere feels unsafe right now…
