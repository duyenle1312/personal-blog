So it’s 2026 and you’re listening to music created by AI, watching a video generated by AI, reading an article created by AI, using a software created by AI, following a recipe for dinner also created by AI, …

i mean seriously people, AI doesn’t generate output by itself. Someone wrote a prompt or created a system to get those output to give it back to you. Stop thinking of AI as an autonomous agency that can act on itself. A human was behind it, AI just helped that human reach you faster and easier. Impacts can be observed in days instead of months or years.
