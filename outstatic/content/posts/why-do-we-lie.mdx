If I say that I no longer love him, that would be a lie. However, sometimes in life, when something hurts you so much, the only thing you can do is to get rid of it before it totally destroys your sanity. So here I am. I wish I never loved him.

I wish we never met. I wish none of this happened. I wish our world never overlapped. I may be able to heal in some other ways. I don’t know. You gave me the world and then just evaporated. It’s like shredding through the illusion that you never chose to be there for me.
